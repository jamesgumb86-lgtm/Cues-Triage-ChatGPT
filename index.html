CUES Triage

Staff decision-support aid for call handlers

Source & scope

This tool follows the CUES Eligibility Screening/Triage script, version 1.0 (20/12/2021). Confirm with your service that this is still the current approved protocol before relying on it — ask the optometrist if in doubt. It is decision support only: some outcomes require discussing the case with a CUES practitioner rather than a fully automated decision. If the patient is feeling generally unwell, advise them to seek medical advice or discuss with the optometrist at the time of booking.

Urgent

Get emergency help now

If this looks like a medical emergency — for example a chemical splash to the eye, a high-velocity injury, or sudden severe vision loss — advise the patient to call 999 or go straight to A&E or the hospital eye service. Do not continue this triage aid for a time-critical situation.
